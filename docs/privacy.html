Privacy Policy

Access App · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Data the app accesses

  • Google account email and name (scopes openid, email, profile). Used only to show which account is connected.
  • Google Drive files created by the app (scope drive.file). The app creates a DriveAccessApp folder containing employees.json and the files you attach. It cannot see or change any other files in your Drive.

Where data is stored

All employee records and attachments are stored only in your own Google Drive. The app has no server; we do not collect, store, sell or share any of your data. Sign-in tokens are stored locally on your computer and are deleted when you sign out.

Use of Google user data

Access App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Click Sign out in the app, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can delete the DriveAccessApp folder from your Drive at any time.
